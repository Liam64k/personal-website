# Sky Isle — personal site design

Date: 2026-11-12. Owner: Liam Stier. Status: approved in conversation ("you may start").

## Intent

Replace the old template site with one page that is a scroll-driven flight over a
realistic floating island. The 3D world, the visuals and the animation are the main
focus. The content rides on top of it.

## Goals

- One page. Scrolling moves a camera through one persistent Three.js world; scrolling
  back rewinds it.
- "Sky Isle" look: bright daytime, realistic rock and grass, soft volumetric-looking
  clouds, sun and haze. The reference is Meng To's code-generated floating island
  (his demo reports 0 image assets).
- Six chapters, in order: Hero, Summit, Workshop, Lab, Towers, Landing.
- Make Liam sound sharp: "AI engineer and founder".

## Non-goals

- No embedded apps or playable demos. Projects are links.
- No multi-page site, blog, or CMS.
- No build step, no framework.
- No phone number anywhere.

## Anti-goals

- The generic AI look: identical cards, all-caps eyebrow labels, arrows on buttons,
  decorative 01/02/03 numbering, gradient blobs.
- Photos that look pasted on. They must sit in the page as part of it.
- A 3D scene that makes the text hard to read.

## Approaches considered

1. **Real-time Three.js, everything generated by code (chosen).** Noise-shaped island,
   GPU grass, sprite clouds, procedural rock shading. Stays interactive, needs no
   accounts or downloads, matches the reference. Realism is "good for a browser", and
   it takes several tuning passes.
2. **Real-time with a downloaded or AI-generated island model.** Higher ceiling for
   rock detail, but needs third-party accounts and a model Liam would have to produce.
   Kept as a later upgrade: the island sits behind one module so it can be swapped.
3. **Pre-rendered video scrubbed by scroll.** Film-level realism, but no real 3D
   interaction, heavy files, and every change means a re-render. Rejected.

## How it is built (my calls)

- Plain files in `src/`, served as-is by Cloudflare. Three.js r170 is vendored in
  `src/vendor/`. No other runtime libraries: the scroll conductor, reveals and camera
  are small hand-written modules.
- One renderer, one scene. Native scroll is the source of truth; a fractional chapter
  value drives camera, light, sky and DOM. Camera is damped; exact progress drives
  navigation and which chapter's text is showing.
- Island: closed mesh from a shared JS height function (top heightfield + craggy
  underside), shaded in the fragment shader by slope and height (grass on flats, rock on
  steeps and below) with procedural bump. Grass, stones and landmarks sample the same
  height function.
- Clouds: instanced soft billboards built from a generated noise texture, sorted back
  to front; a cloud sea below, cumulus heaps around, a layer the camera sinks through.
- Light moves with the journey: midday at the top, golden hour at the landing.
- Landmarks: summit cairn and flag; workshop cabin with chimney smoke and a hanging
  heavy bag; lab of seven screen slabs; four beacon towers; a stone jetty with lanterns.
- Text is real HTML above the canvas on a soft cloud-white haze, dark ink type.
  Fraunces for display, Hanken Grotesk for text.
- Photos are treated as physical prints (white border, slight tilt). The desk photo
  sits in the hero; the Muay Thai photo opens in a dialog from the Workshop chapter.
- Phones: fewer blades and puffs, lower pixel ratio, camera pulled back.
  `prefers-reduced-motion`: camera snaps between chapters, ambient motion off.
  No WebGL: a CSS sky and the full readable page.

## Left to the builder

Exact camera keyframes, colour values, densities and timing, tuned by screenshot.

## Verification

Screenshots of every chapter at desktop and phone size; scroll forward, back and jump;
console clean; reduced-motion and no-WebGL paths; one independent adversarial review
against every request in the conversation.

## Revision 2 (2026-11-12): more realism, less bland

Asked for after the first build: smoother and more realistic visuals, a better
workshop, more clouds and islands, and the fitness facts moved out of the intro.

- A finishing pass (`world/post.js`): multisampled HDR buffer, bloom, sun shafts,
  film tone curve, vignette. `?nopost` turns it off; `?msaa=N` sets the sample count.
- Fine rock detail and thin grass blades fade or widen with distance, which removes
  the grainy look at range.
- The cabin is replaced by a round stone forge-tower with a green copper roof, a
  copper flue, a forge mouth and a wind rotor.
- New: a great blossom tree with falling petals, groves, vines under the rim, nine
  more islets (four large, with trees), drifting rock, thunderheads on the horizon,
  birds, fireflies at dusk, and an airship moored at the landing.
- Content: Summit is now a short placeholder intro. The gym, Muay Thai, rock
  climbing and the 50 km run sit under Online personal training, with the photo.
